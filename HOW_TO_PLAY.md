# How to play The Long Game

*[Leer en español](HOW_TO_PLAY.es.md)*

A guide for players who know Unciv (or Civ V). It covers only what's **different**. Every system also has an article
in the in-game Civilopedia, and every tech, building and event choice states its exact effect.

## The short version

1. **Do things to research faster.** Most techs have an *Insight*: build 2 Mines, meet a civ, defeat a unit… and that
   tech becomes 33% cheaper to research. The condition is in the tech's description.
2. **Build Quarters.** Light districts: pick a tile, it yields without a worker. A city holds one Quarter per 4 Population.
   Later buildings (University, Bank, Workshop, Grand Temple) need the matching Quarter.
3. **Pick a Legacy each era.** A permanent bonus in your capital; your choices add up to your civ's identity.
4. **Wars cost something.** *War Weariness* grows while you fight and makes your people unhappy. Losing a city or being
   attacked gives you comeback tools.
5. **Expanding has diminishing returns.** *Imperial Strain* makes techs, policies and happiness costlier when you have
   far more cities than your rivals.
6. **Read the popups.** A welcome note on turn 1 and short notes the first time Strain or War Weariness appear explain
   the basics. Events ask for decisions: declaring war, conquering, alliances, golden ages, unrest, natural wonders.

## Your first 30 turns

- **Palisade** (no tech, 35 Production): +3 city strength, +25 HP. Cities are also stronger from the start. Early rushes
  are harder, so don't build only military.
- **Slingers** are cheap early ranged units; they upgrade to Archers.
- Your **first city triggers the Founding Myth**: Warrior Clans (military), Master Builders (production) or Mystic Visions
  (faith and culture).
- Early Insights come naturally: found a 2nd city (Pottery), meet a civ (Writing), build a Palisade (Masonry), own
  3 Hills (Mining), defeat a unit (Archery). Plan your first techs around what you'll do anyway.
- Techs cost 33% more than vanilla. Collecting Insights pays it back.

## Insights (tech boosts)

- Every tech except Agriculture and Future Tech (78 in all) has one Insight that makes it **33% cheaper** to research.
- Checked at the start of every turn, only for techs you haven't researched yet. Each one works once, and a popup
  tells you when you earn it.
- You can earn an Insight long before you research the tech: the discount waits for you and shows in the research cost.
- **Banked Insights are listed in your capital's buildings** as "Insight: <tech>" until you research the tech.
- Techs cost **+33%**. With about three quarters of the Insights you research at vanilla speed; with all of them
  about 10% faster; ignoring them is a third slower.
- **Tech screen quirk:** while you research a tech that has an Insight, the tech screen shows *every* tech with that
  discount. Select a tech to see its real cost. The research itself is always correct.
- Many later Insights use the new systems: Guilds ← an Artisan Quarter, Education ← 3 Academy Quarters,
  Architecture ← own 6 Quarters, Gunpowder ← 5 victories, Rifling ← 10 victories.

## Quarters (light districts)

Built like a building (1 Gold upkeep). You choose the tile, which can't have a resource. The tile **yields without a
citizen working it**. A city has **one Quarter Slot for every 4 Population** (4, 8, 12…), and each Quarter uses one, so
pick the ones the city needs.

| Quarter | Tech | Base | Bonus | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Academy Quarter | Writing | +2 Science | +1 per adjacent Mountain | University |
| Market Quarter | Currency | +2 Gold | +1 per adjacent luxury resource | Bank |
| Sacred Grove | Philosophy | +2 Faith, +1 Culture | +1 Faith per adjacent Forest | Grand Temple |
| Artisan Quarter | Construction | +2 Production | +1 per adjacent Mine | Workshop |
| Garrison Quarter | Bronze Working | +50% defence on its tile; adjacent enemies take 10 damage per turn | — | +2 unit supply, +15% military production in the city |

Every Quarter (except the Garrison) also gets **+2 next to a natural wonder**, and adds 1 Great Person point per turn
(Academy: Scientist, Market: Merchant, Artisan: Engineer, Sacred Grove: Artist). Quarters can be pillaged, which makes raids hurt.

## Legacies (one choice per era)

Entering a new era (and founding your first city) offers three permanent bonuses. They live in a building in your
capital and stack over the game. **Rising Star**: if you're more than 2 techs behind the average, a 4th option gives a
free technology instead.

| Era | Military | Economy | Culture / Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding Myth | Warrior Clans | Master Builders | Mystic Visions |
| Classical | Legions | Bread and Circuses | Lyceum |
| Medieval | Chivalry | Guilds | Pilgrimage |
| Renaissance | Conquistadors | Banking | Patronage |
| Industrial | Conscription | Factory System | Public Schools |
| Modern | Total Mobilization | Consumerism | Scientific Method |
| Atomic | Deterrence | Welfare State | Space Race |
| Information | Cyber Warfare | Globalization | Digital Culture |

## Imperial Strain (expansion)

Strain (top bar) = your cities beyond 125% of what the other civs average, with one city free. Each point makes techs
**+4%**, policies **+5%** and unhappiness from the number of cities **+10%** more expensive. Wide empires still win
more, just with diminishing returns. Strain also blocks *Rally the Nation*, which is for the underdog.

## War

**War Weariness** (top bar):
- **Gained:** +2 per turn for each major civ you're at war with, +5 for declaring war, +1 per military unit lost, +3 per city
  you conquer, +5 per city you lose. The per-turn rate is scaled by game speed.
- **Lost:** −3 per turn at peace (scaled), and making peace halves it.
- **Effect:** every 5 points add +5% unhappiness from population, up to +50%.
- **Helps:** Honor, the Aztecs, the Great Bath, the Pax Dei belief, the Total Mobilization Legacy.

When you **declare war**, choose a **Casus Belli**:
- *War Fervor*: new units +15 XP for 8 turns. +2 War Weariness.
- *Blitz*: +1 Movement for military land units for 3 turns. +2 War Weariness.
- *Propaganda*: pay 60 Gold for −5 War Weariness.

When **someone declares war on you** (and you have no Strain): **Call to Arms**, +25% military production for 6 turns.

When you **conquer a city**:
- *Plunder*: +100 Gold, +3 War Weariness.
- *Integrate*: pay 50 Gold for −3 War Weariness.
- *Garrison*: a free defensive unit of your era.

When you **lose a city**: **Rally the Nation** (once every 15 turns, not while you have Strain):
- *Levée en masse*: 2 free defensive units.
- *Scorched Earth*: +20% strength in your own territory for 10 turns.
- *Call the Generals*: a free Great General.

When a **war ends**: **Reconstruction**, +10% Production for 10 turns.

Always on:
- **Desperate Defence**: +10% strength when defending against a civ with more cities than you.

All durations above are for Standard speed and scale with game speed.

## Armies

- **Battering Ram** (Bronze Working) and **Siege Tower** (Physics): civilian support units. Adjacent melee land units
  get +30% / +40% against cities. Escort them: they can be captured.
- **Field Medic** (Chemistry): adjacent units heal +15 HP.
- **Mercenary Company** (Civil Service): bought only with Gold, ready to move at once, 50% higher upkeep.
- **Morale**:
  - Losing 50+ HP in one attack makes a unit *Shaken*: −15% for 1 turn.
  - Defeating a military unit makes it *Emboldened*: +10% for 2 turns.
- Unit upkeep grows 5% per era, so big standing armies get more expensive over time.

## Happiness and Unrest

At **−5 Happiness or worse**, **Unrest** breaks out (at most every 15 turns):
- *Martial Law*: +2 Happiness in every city with a military unit inside, −10% Production, no rebels; lasts 10 turns.
- *Concessions*: 15 Gold per city for +1 Happiness in every city; lasts 10 turns.
- *Ignore them*: free, but 50% chance of rebels near your capital.

Vanilla still applies below −10 (no growth, −50% Production, −33% unit strength) and spawns rebels below −20.

## Other events

- **Alliance** (declaring friendship or a defensive pact), one perk for 20 turns:
  - *Joint Research*: +5% Science.
  - *Arms Exchange*: new units +10 XP.
  - *Trade Compact*: +1 Gold per city.
- **Golden Age**, one focus for its duration:
  - *Arts*: +20% Culture.
  - *Industry*: +15% Production.
  - *Learning*: +15% Science.
- **Expedition** (you discover a natural wonder). Amounts double from the Renaissance on:
  - *Survey*: 40 Science.
  - *Pilgrimage*: 20 Faith and 15 Culture.
  - *Tall Tales*: 100 Gold.

## Natural wonders

Worth settling for:
- Their tile yields **without a citizen working it**, and the yields are bigger than in vanilla.
- Each natural wonder tile you own gives **+2 Happiness**.
- Quarters next to one get **+2** of their yield (the Sacred Grove +2 Faith).

Each one also has a **signature effect**. Those marked * are **permanent promotions**, like the Fountain of Youth's
Rejuvenation: any unit (from any civilization) that moves next to the wonder keeps it for the rest of the game.

| Natural wonder | Signature effect |
|---|---|
| Great Barrier Reef | *Coral Reef*: any unit that ends its turn next to it, even yours, takes 20 damage |
| Krakatoa | *Fire Mountain*: enemy units that end their turn next to it, inside the borders of a civilization at war with them, take 30 damage |
| Old Faithful | *Hot Springs*: units that end their turn next to it heal 20 HP, even after moving or fighting |
| Fountain of Youth | *Rejuvenation\**: all healing doubled (military land units) |
| Mount Sinai | *Revelation\**: +50% XP from combat (military units) |
| Mount Kailash | *Altitude Training\**: double movement and +10% Strength in Hills (military land units) |
| Rock of Gibraltar | *Pillars of Hercules\**: +1 Movement (military naval units) |
| Grand Mesa | *Vantage\**: +1 Sight (land units) |
| Mount Fuji | *Spirit of Fuji\**: no strength penalty when wounded (military land units) |
| Barringer Crater | *Star Metal\**: +10% Strength (melee land units) |
| Uluru | *Songlines\**: ignores Zone of Control (military land units) |
| Sri Pada | *Pilgrim's Path\**: +1 Movement (civilian units: Workers, Settlers, Great People, missionaries) |
| Cerro de Potosi | *Silver Mountain*: while you own it, all your Mines yield +1 Gold |
| El Dorado | *City of Gold*: 500 Gold to the first civilization to discover it |

## Civilizations

Every civilization has a **unique ability** and **4 unique components** (units, buildings, improvements or a unique
Quarter). Each component replaces a standard one; the Civilopedia shows the exact numbers.

- **Rome** (Augustus Caesar). Imperial Strain hurts about half as much; +25% Production for buildings your capital already has.
  Legion (Swordsman: 17 strength, builds roads and forts) · Ballista (Catapult: 8/10 strength) · Castrum (Garrison Quarter: units trained in the city start with +10 XP) · Basilica (Courthouse: any city, +1 Culture, +1 Happiness)
- **Korea** (Sejong). +1 Science per specialist; Academy Quarters yield +50%.
  Hwach'a (Trebuchet: 26 ranged strength) · Turtle Ship (Caravel: 36 strength, coast only) · Jiphyeonjeon (Academy Quarter: +1 Culture on the tile, 2 Great Scientist points) · Sanseong (Castle: stronger, adjacent units heal +10)
- **Egypt** (Ramesses II). +20% Production towards wonders; Artisan Quarters yield +50%.
  War Chariot (Chariot Archer: no Horses needed, 5 Movement) · Burial Tomb (Temple: +2 Happiness, no upkeep, but double gold if captured) · Deir el-Medina (Artisan Quarter: +15% wonder Production in the city) · Medjay (Spearman: 12 strength, +1 Sight, +25% in Desert)
- **Aztecs** (Montezuma I). Culture from every kill; War Weariness recovers 1 faster per turn.
  Jaguar (Warrior: strong in Forest and Jungle) · Floating Gardens (Water Mill: +2 Food on Lakes) · Tlachtli (Circus: +1 Faith, +1 Culture) · Teocalli (Sacred Grove: +2 Culture on the tile)
- **Byzantium** (Theodora). One extra belief when founding a religion; Sacred Groves yield +50%.
  Dromon (Trireme: ranged, +50% vs ships) · Cataphract (Horseman: 15 strength) · Varangian Guard (Longswordsman: +20% defending, +10% next to a Great General) · Katholikon (Temple: +1 Culture, 1 upkeep)
- **Babylon** (Nebuchadnezzar II). Insights make techs 50% cheaper instead of 33%; Great Scientists 33% faster.
  Bowman (Archer: 7/9 strength) · Walls of Babylon (Walls: double HP) · Edubba (Library: +1 Culture, 1 Great Scientist point) · Ziggurat (Shrine: +1 Science)
- **Mapuche** (Lautaro). +20% strength against civs with more cities; units heal 10 HP after a kill.
  Malón Raider (Knight: pillage specialist) · Malal (Palisade: +5 strength, +40 HP) · Kona (Spearman: double movement in Forest, +15% in own land) · Koyagtun (Amphitheater: +1 Happiness, less unhappiness at war)
- **Vietnam** (Bà Triệu). +25% strength in own Forest, Jungle and Marsh; enemies spend 1 extra movement in your land.
  Voi Chiến (Crossbowman: 15 strength, 3 Movement, can move after attacking) · Đình (Monument: +1 Happiness) · Tây Sơn Musketeer (Musketman: double movement in Jungle and Forest) · Rice Paddy (improvement on Marsh and Flood plains: +2 Food, +1 Gold)
- **Mali** (Mansa Musa). Market Quarters yield double; +1 Gold on Desert; −10% building Production.
  Suguba (Market: +3 Gold, +1 Faith) · Mandekalu Cavalry (Knight: Gold from kills) · Sankore (University: +1 Faith, +2 Gold) · Griot House (Amphitheater: +4 Culture, 1 Great Artist point)
- **Poland** (Casimir III). Picks two Legacies every era.
  Winged Hussar (Lancer: 28 strength, flanking expert) · Sukiennice (Market: +3 Gold, +1 Culture) · Hajduk (Musketman: 26 strength, +10% attacking) · Sejm (Courthouse: any city, +2 Culture)
- **Persia** (Darius I). Golden Ages last 50% longer; during them +1 Movement and +10% strength.
  Persian Immortal (Spearman: 12 strength, heals faster) · Satrap's Court (Bank: +1 Happiness) · Pairidaeza (Garden: +1 Culture, +1 Happiness) · Qanat (improvement on Desert/Plains next to a Hill: +2 Food)
- **Inca** (Pachacuti). Hills cost no extra movement; +15% strength on Hills; road upkeep −50%.
  Waraka (Slinger: 7 ranged strength, +1 Sight) · Terrace farm (Hill farm: +1 Food per adjacent Mountain) · Intihuatana (Sacred Grove: +2 Faith per adjacent Mountain) · Mit'a Laborer (Worker: builds 25% faster)
- **Sweden** (Gustavus Adolphus). Alliance perks last 40 turns instead of 20; declaring friendship gives both sides +10% Great Person generation; Great Generals +1 Movement.
  Carolean (Rifleman: +15% attacking) · Hakkapeliitta (Lancer: +1 Movement) · Bruk (Forge: +1 Production, +1 Production on Mines) · Regalskepp (Frigate: 30 ranged strength, +1 Sight)
- **The Ottomans** (Suleiman I). Conquering a city causes no War Weariness; −50% unhappiness from population in annexed cities.
  Janissary (Musketman: +25% attacking, heals 50 HP after a kill) · Sipahi (Lancer: +1 Movement, +1 Sight, free pillaging) · Great Bombard (Cannon: 23 ranged strength) · Hammam (Aqueduct: +1 Happiness)
- **Zulu** (Shaka). Never Shaken; promotions need 25% less XP; melee units −50% upkeep.
  Impi (Pikeman: +1 Movement) · Ikanda (Barracks: +1 unit supply, +15% melee Production) · Isibaya (Pasture: +1 Production) · Isijula Thrower (Archer: 8 ranged strength)

## Policies, religion and wonders

- **Piety** is no longer exclusive with Rationalism:
  - The opener also gives +1 Culture per Shrine.
  - Theocracy gives +1 Happiness per Temple.
  - Completing the branch gives a Great Prophet.
- **Honor** gives Culture for killing any military unit, not just barbarians, and War Weariness recovers faster.
- **Liberty**: Citizenship also gives +1 Happiness per Quarter, Meritocracy −10% unhappiness from population.
- **Freedom**: Constitution also gives +2 Culture per Great Person improvement.
- **Naval Tradition** adds +2 Gold in coastal cities.
- University +25% and Research Lab +40% Science (were +33% / +50%).
- **Beliefs:** weak pantheons and beliefs were buffed. Peace Loving and World Church also count your own followers. New beliefs:
  - *Star Gazers* (pantheon): every Insight also gives 10 Faith.
  - *Holy Groves*: Sacred Grove tiles +1 Faith, +1 Culture.
  - *Warrior Monks*: new military units +10 XP.
  - *Pax Dei*: War Weariness recovers faster, Culture per follower city at peace.
- **New wonders:**
  - *Great Bath*: War Weariness −2 per turn.
  - *Hadrian's Wall*: enemy land units spend 1 extra movement in your territory, +15% city strength.
  - *House of Wisdom*: every Insight also gives 30 Culture.
  - *Grand Bazaar*: Market Quarter tiles +2 Gold, +1 Culture.
  - *Palace of Versailles*: Golden Ages last 25% longer, and your Golden Age focus is twice as strong.
  - *Crystal Palace*: choose one extra Legacy in every later era.
- **Wonder tax:** each World Wonder you own makes the next one 5% slower to build.

## Multiplayer tips

- **1v1:** Strain compares you only to your opponent, so out-expanding them by a lot costs you. Rome and Mapuche handle size
  differences best, from opposite sides.
- **Free-for-all:** declaring war costs War Weariness on every front. Alliances give perks, and defensive pacts make
  dogpiling the leader easier.
- **Teams:** the Alliance event rewards friendships and defensive pacts with your teammates.
- Agree on the game speed. **Very Quick** (0.5×) is supported, and all timers and rates scale with speed.
- Use the same mod version for everyone, and don't update mid-game.

## Optional: The Long Game Fortunes

A separate extension with random events (plague, harvests, droughts, gold rushes, comets, pirate raids, mercenaries,
wandering scholars, great fires, heresies). About one every 25 turns on Standard, never before turn 10. Each one fits
your situation and asks for a choice. Enable it per game if your group likes surprises.
