# How to play The Long Game

*[Leer en español](HOW_TO_PLAY.es.md)*

A guide for players who know Unciv (or Civ V). It covers only what's **different**. Every system also has an article
in the in-game Civilopedia, and every tech, building and event choice states its exact effect.

## The short version

1. **Do things to research faster.** Most techs have an *Insight*: build 2 Mines, meet a civ, defeat a unit… and you
   get 40% of that tech for free. The condition is in the tech's description.
2. **Build Quarters.** Light districts: pick a tile, it yields without a worker, and its neighbours add to it. Later
   buildings (University, Bank, Workshop, Grand Temple) need the matching Quarter.
3. **Pick a Legacy each era.** A permanent bonus in your capital; your choices add up to your civ's identity.
4. **Wars cost something.** *War Weariness* grows while you fight and makes your people unhappy. Losing a city or being
   attacked gives you comeback tools.
5. **Expanding has diminishing returns.** *Imperial Strain* makes techs, policies and happiness costlier when you have
   far more cities than your rivals.
6. **Read the popups.** A welcome note on turn 1 and short notes the first time Strain or War Weariness appear explain
   the basics. Events ask for decisions: declaring war, conquering, alliances, golden ages, unrest, natural wonders.

## Your first 30 turns

- **Palisade** (no tech, 35 Production): +3 city strength, +25 HP. Cities are also stronger from the start. Early rushes
  are harder, so don't build only military.
- **Slingers** are cheap early ranged units; they upgrade to Archers.
- Your **first city triggers the Founding Myth**: Warrior Clans (military), Master Builders (production) or Mystic Visions
  (faith and culture).
- Early Insights come naturally: found a 2nd city (Pottery), meet a civ (Writing), build a Monument (Masonry), own
  3 Hills (Mining), defeat a unit (Archery). Plan your first techs around what you'll do anyway.
- Early techs cost 25% more than vanilla. Collecting Insights more than pays it back.

## Insights (tech boosts)

- Every tech except Agriculture and Future Tech (78 in all) has one Insight worth **40%** of the tech.
- Checked whenever you finish a tech or any city finishes a building. Each one fires once.
- Techs cost **+25%**. Getting about half the Insights puts you at vanilla speed; getting most
  of them is faster; ignoring them is slower.
- Many later Insights use the new systems: Guilds ← an Artisan Quarter, Education ← 2 Academy Quarters,
  Architecture ← own 4 Quarters, Gunpowder ← 5 victories, Rifling ← 10 victories.

## Quarters (light districts)

Built like a building (city of 3+ population, 1 Gold upkeep). You choose the tile, which can't have a resource.
The tile **yields without a citizen working it**, and each adjacent feature adds +1.

| Quarter | Tech | Base | +1 for each adjacent | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Academy Quarter | Writing | +1 Science | Mountain, Jungle, Quarter | University |
| Market Quarter | Currency | +1 Gold | River, luxury resource, Quarter | Bank |
| Sacred Grove | Philosophy | +1 Faith, +1 Culture | Forest, Mountain, Quarter (+2 Faith per Natural Wonder) | Grand Temple |
| Artisan Quarter | Construction | +1 Production | Mine, Quarry, Lumber mill, Quarter | Workshop |
| Garrison Quarter | Bronze Working | +50% defence on its tile; adjacent enemies take 10 damage per turn | — | +2 unit supply, +15% military production in the city |

**Great People love Quarters.** Each Quarter except the Garrison adds 1 Great Person point per turn (Academy: Scientist,
Market: Merchant, Artisan: Engineer, Sacred Grove: Artist), and gets **+2** for every adjacent Great Person improvement
(Academy, Manufactory, Customs house, Landmark…).

Tips: Quarters next to each other boost each other, so cluster them, and plant your Great People next to them. They can be
pillaged, which makes raids hurt.

## Legacies (one choice per era)

Entering a new era (and founding your first city) offers three permanent bonuses. They live in a building in your
capital and stack over the game. **Rising Star**: if you're more than 2 techs behind the average, a 4th option gives a
free technology instead.

| Era | Military | Economy | Culture / Science |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founding Myth | Warrior Clans | Master Builders | Mystic Visions |
| Classical | Legions | Bread and Circuses | Lyceum |
| Medieval | Chivalry | Guilds | Pilgrimage |
| Renaissance | Conquistadors | Banking | Patronage |
| Industrial | Conscription | Factory System | Public Schools |
| Modern | Total Mobilization | Consumerism | Scientific Method |
| Atomic | Deterrence | Welfare State | Space Race |
| Information | Cyber Warfare | Globalization | Digital Culture |

## Imperial Strain (expansion)

Strain (top bar) = your cities beyond 125% of what the other civs average, with one city free. Each point makes techs
**+4%**, policies **+5%** and unhappiness from the number of cities **+10%** more expensive. Wide empires still win
more, just with diminishing returns. Strain also blocks *Rally the Nation*, which is for the underdog.

## War

**War Weariness** (top bar):
- **Gained:** +2 per turn for each major civ you're at war with, +5 for declaring war, +1 per military unit lost, +3 per city
  you conquer, +5 per city you lose. The per-turn rate is scaled by game speed.
- **Lost:** −3 per turn at peace (scaled), and making peace halves it.
- **Effect:** every 5 points add +5% unhappiness from population, up to +50%.
- **Helps:** Honor, the Aztecs, the Great Bath, the Pax Dei belief, the Total Mobilization Legacy.

When you **declare war**, choose a **Casus Belli**:
- *War Fervor*: new units +15 XP for 8 turns. +2 War Weariness.
- *Blitz*: +1 Movement for military land units for 3 turns. +2 War Weariness.
- *Propaganda*: pay 60 Gold for −5 War Weariness.

When **someone declares war on you** (and you have no Strain): **Call to Arms**, +25% military production for 6 turns.

When you **conquer a city**:
- *Plunder*: +100 Gold, +3 War Weariness.
- *Integrate*: pay 50 Gold for −3 War Weariness.
- *Garrison*: a free defensive unit of your era.

When you **lose a city**: **Rally the Nation** (once every 15 turns, not while you have Strain):
- *Levée en masse*: 2 free defensive units.
- *Scorched Earth*: +20% strength in your own territory for 10 turns.
- *Call the Generals*: a free Great General.

When a **war ends**: **Reconstruction**, +10% Production for 10 turns.

Always on:
- **Desperate Defence**: +10% strength when defending against a civ with more cities than you.

All durations above are for Standard speed and scale with game speed.

## Armies

- **Battering Ram** (Bronze Working) and **Siege Tower** (Physics): civilian support units. Adjacent melee land units
  get +30% / +40% against cities. Escort them: they can be captured.
- **Field Medic** (Chemistry): adjacent units heal +15 HP.
- **Mercenary Company** (Civil Service): bought only with Gold, ready to move at once, 50% higher upkeep.
- **Morale**:
  - Losing 50+ HP in one attack makes a unit *Shaken*: −15% for 1 turn.
  - Defeating a military unit makes it *Emboldened*: +10% for 2 turns.
- Unit upkeep grows 5% per era, so big standing armies get more expensive over time.

## Happiness and Unrest

At **−5 Happiness or worse**, **Unrest** breaks out (at most every 15 turns):
- *Martial Law*: +2 Happiness in every city with a military unit inside, −10% Production, no rebels; lasts 10 turns.
- *Concessions*: 15 Gold per city for +1 Happiness in every city; lasts 10 turns.
- *Ignore them*: free, but 50% chance of rebels near your capital.

Vanilla still applies below −10 (no growth, −50% Production, −33% unit strength) and spawns rebels below −20.

## Other events

- **Alliance** (declaring friendship or a defensive pact), one perk for 20 turns:
  - *Joint Research*: +5% Science.
  - *Arms Exchange*: new units +10 XP.
  - *Trade Compact*: +1 Gold per city.
- **Golden Age**, one focus for its duration:
  - *Arts*: +20% Culture.
  - *Industry*: +15% Production.
  - *Learning*: +15% Science.
- **Expedition** (you discover a natural wonder). Amounts double from the Renaissance on:
  - *Survey*: Science.
  - *Pilgrimage*: Faith and Culture.
  - *Tall Tales*: Gold.

## Policies, religion and wonders

- **Piety** is no longer exclusive with Rationalism:
  - The opener also gives +1 Culture per Shrine.
  - Theocracy gives +1 Happiness per Temple.
  - Completing the branch gives a Great Prophet.
- **Honor** gives Culture for killing any military unit, not just barbarians, and War Weariness recovers faster.
- **Liberty**: Citizenship also gives +1 Happiness per Quarter, Meritocracy −10% unhappiness from population.
- **Freedom**: Constitution also gives +2 Culture per Great Person improvement.
- **Naval Tradition** adds +2 Gold in coastal cities.
- University +25% and Research Lab +40% Science (were +33% / +50%).
- **Beliefs:** weak pantheons and beliefs were buffed. Peace Loving and World Church also count your own followers. New beliefs:
  - *Star Gazers* (pantheon): every Insight also gives 25 Faith.
  - *Holy Groves*: Sacred Grove tiles +1 Faith, +1 Culture.
  - *Warrior Monks*: new military units +10 XP.
  - *Pax Dei*: War Weariness recovers faster, Culture per follower city at peace.
- **New wonders:**
  - *Great Bath*: War Weariness −2 per turn.
  - *Hadrian's Wall*: enemy land units spend 1 extra movement in your territory, +15% city strength.
  - *House of Wisdom*: every Insight also gives 30 Culture.
  - *Grand Bazaar*: Market Quarter tiles +2 Gold, +1 Culture.
  - *Palace of Versailles*: Golden Ages last 25% longer, and your Golden Age focus is twice as strong.
  - *Crystal Palace*: choose one extra Legacy in every later era.
- **Wonder tax:** each World Wonder you own makes the next one 5% slower to build.

## Multiplayer tips

- **1v1:** Strain compares you only to your opponent, so out-expanding them by a lot costs you. Rome and Mapuche handle size
  differences best, from opposite sides.
- **Free-for-all:** declaring war costs War Weariness on every front. Alliances give perks, and defensive pacts make
  dogpiling the leader easier.
- **Teams:** the Alliance event rewards friendships and defensive pacts with your teammates.
- Agree on the game speed. **Very Quick** (0.5×) is supported, and all timers and rates scale with speed.
- Use the same mod version for everyone, and don't update mid-game.

## Optional: The Long Game Fortunes

A separate extension with random events (plague, harvests, droughts, gold rushes, comets, pirate raids, mercenaries,
wandering scholars, great fires, heresies). About one every 25 turns on Standard, never before turn 10. Each one fits
your situation and asks for a choice. Enable it per game if your group likes surprises.
